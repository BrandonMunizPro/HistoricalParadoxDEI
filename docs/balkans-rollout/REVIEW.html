Ownership isolation diagnostic — deliberately nonhistorical

Aetolian territory temporarily shares the Antigonid neighbour's owner, then only Aetolia changes to Rome. Geometry and rasters stay fixed; one palette entry changes; the neighbouring pixels stay unchanged. Original owners are restored. These images are test evidence, not a proposed campaign setup.

Inspection still required: source boundary credibility, coastline/terrain contacts, close vegetation noise, zoom transitions, label density and interactive performance. The Italy/Sicily controls have visually unchanged completed-area crops (nine and eight pixels differ by one 8-bit channel level); their full frames also see newly treated neighbouring geography near the right horizon. Rendering cost remains higher: the eight new-area captures average roughly 24–38 ms versus 4–12 ms before. These are submitted timings, not accepted interactive FPS. Performance needs profiling before this becomes the template for later regions. No global rollout is included.
