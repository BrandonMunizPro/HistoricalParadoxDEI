# Faction placement review — 278 BCE campaign

The Italian Boii should remain around Bologna, south of the Po. Moving this
faction to Bohemia to put it north of the Taurisci would remove the well-attested
Italian group. Polybius, Histories 2.17, distinguishes the Boii south of the Po
from the Veneti towards the Adriatic; 2.20 describes Boii fighting Rome shortly
before the campaign date. This supports the broad placement, not our drawn border.

Strabo 4.6.9 associates the Taurisci with the Norici near the inner Adriatic.
His account is much later and cannot authenticate the scenario's 278 BCE polygon.
Bohemian Boii are a different geographical question: their relationship to the
Italian group does not imply a single continuous state spanning both regions.

## Gaul and Britain

Most named tribal regions here are already marked `provisional`. Familiar late
Iron Age names and geographical associations do not establish their sovereignty
or exact borders in 278 BCE. The British Catuvellauni especially need a dated
research pass before being presented as an attested state at this start date.
[English Heritage's archaeological account](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/lexden-earthworks-and-bluebottle-grove/History/iron-age-kings/)
places the relevant written evidence and oppida in a substantially later period.
The [University of Warwick's tribal overview](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/arts/classics/warwickclassicsnetwork/romancoventry/resources/prehistoricbritain/ironage/tribes/)
is useful for broad regional associations, not for backdating those boundaries.

`tools/faction-placement-audit.mjs` records every displayed faction label against
its own scenario territories. This is a geometric consistency audit, not a claim
that all 131 placements have been historically confirmed. No faction, ownership
or canonical settlement records were moved by this terrain pass.
