# Vertical slices and epic ordering

- Canonical version: [vertical-slices-and-epics.md](vertical-slices-and-epics.md)

Dependency-ordered epics E0–E18 (with persistence split into E17a and E17b) and
vertical slices S1–S12. The slices added by the time and performance decisions
are *Simultaneous Living World* and *Bounded Computation*; those added by the
cohesion, displacement, political-transformation and presentation decisions are
*Displacement and Migration*, *Political Transformation*, and *Culturally
Distinct but Shared*. PT1 (presentation) is a parallel track outside the
dependency chain. No timelines; ordering is dependency-driven.
