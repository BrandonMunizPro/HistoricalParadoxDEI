# R-16 regional coverage matrix

**Status: Proposal (2026-10-14), part of R-16.** Tracks **what is researched, for
which entities, across the ten research areas** — so gaps are visible and the
program stays honest about progress. Complements
[`r16-candidate-entity-inventory.md`](r16-candidate-entity-inventory.md) (the
*who*) and [`r16-world-political-institutions-program.md`](r16-world-political-institutions-program.md)
(the method).

## Legend

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ✔ | Covered by a written sheet |
| ◐ | Partially covered (present but not explicit) |
| ▢ | Planned, not yet written |
| ~ | Deliberately **representative-pattern** only (T3), not a bespoke sheet |
| — | Not applicable / out of scope for this tier |

## The ten areas (column keys)

**1** Government structure · **2** Offices/appointments · **3** Succession/elections/terms ·
**4** Legal authority/checks · **5** Informal influence · **6** Military command ·
**7** Treasury/tax/spending · **8** Citizenship/classes/participation ·
**9** Subordinate communities · **10** Evidence/uncertainty/anachronism.

> **Note on the three first-pass sheets (Rome, Carthage, Ptolemaic Egypt):** they
> were written on the earlier A–P template and **retrofitted 2026-10-14** with
> explicit **§J.1** citizenship/social-classes and **§J.2** subordinate-communities
> blocks. All ten areas are now covered (✔).
