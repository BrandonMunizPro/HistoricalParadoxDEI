# R-16 candidate entity inventory (278 BCE)

**Status: Proposal (2026-10-14), part of R-16.** The **discovery + verification**
output of the world program: every political entity discoverable on the intended
map (via the *Rome II* grand-campaign roster and the **DeI** featured-faction
index), grouped geographically, each **independently classified for 278 BCE**.
Discovery references are **not** authorities: game names and borders are never
copied into the start-world.

Related: [`r16-world-political-institutions-program.md`](r16-world-political-institutions-program.md)
(method, classes, priorities), [`r16-regional-coverage-matrix.md`](r16-regional-coverage-matrix.md),
[`../architecture/start-world-research-plan.md`](../architecture/start-world-research-plan.md).

## How to read this

- **Reference** column = the game reference used only to *find* the candidate:
  `(P)` Rome II playable, `(DeI)` DeI featured, `(GC)` Rome II grand-campaign
  minor faction, or a named city/people with no direct game faction.
- **Class** = **A** established independent state · **B** dependent/subordinate ·
  **C** confederation/league · **D** community/people/ethnos · **E** later
  historical formation (anachronism) · **F** uncertain / `needs-research`.
- **Conf.** = Verified · Likely · Uncertain · Anachronistic label.
- This inventory is the **entity spine for R-13** and the **grouping for the
  coverage matrix**. It asserts no border: a class-D people has a *name and a
  rough area*, not a frontier.

> **Not entities (excluded).** Rome II "Secessionists", "Rebels", "[DO NOT
> LOCALISE]", "Slaves", "Nova Carthago" (as a *polity*) and similar are game
> constructs or generic rebel tags, not 278 polities. Where a real people
> underlies them it is listed directly.

## 13. Open verification gaps (class F, `needs-research`)

- **Britain/Ireland** entity names and any 278 political structure (thin record).
- **Sahara** polities (Garamantes' 278 state-form; "Libya" as anything other than
  a people).
- **Inner Germania** at 278 (essentially undocumented).
- **Mascat/Omana** and Gulf polities besides Gerrha/Dilmun.
- The **Iberian** and **Gallic** peoples' names at 278 (mostly attested later).
- The **state-form** of Noricum and the Balkan Celtic polities at 278.

> This inventory is deliberately **complete rather than precise**: it exists so
> nothing on the map is silently imported, and so the uncertain rows are visible
> as `needs-research` rather than becoming false canon.
