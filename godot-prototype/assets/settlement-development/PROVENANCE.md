# Settlement development vertical slice

All twelve near/far native meshes are original project geometry authored in
`settlement_development_kit.gd`, extending the existing original Roma mesh tools.
No Total War, DEI, Medieval Kingdoms or 0 A.D. meshes or code were imported.

Materials reference the existing Roma showcase's CC0 Poly Haven plaster, stone,
timber and terracotta maps. Exact upstream sources, author names, hashes and
licenses are retained in [the inherited manifest](../roma-showcase/PROVENANCE.md)
and that folder's download manifests. No additional downloads or purchases were
required. The surrounding trees still use the previously disclosed generated
branch texture; no new AI imagery was created for this slice.

The Forum's Curia/comitium/rostra/taberna arrangement is an interpretation of
Republican civic functions, not a measured reconstruction of 278 BCE. The
central civic anchor comes from the existing showcase. Forum Level III is a
game design classification. Levels I/II are comparison forms, not claims of
three literal historical construction phases. Grain-storage massing and its
starting level/location are explicitly interpretive provisioning examples.

Other cultural families have design specifications and eligibility definitions,
not finished art. Missing compatible meshes remain explicit asset gaps.
