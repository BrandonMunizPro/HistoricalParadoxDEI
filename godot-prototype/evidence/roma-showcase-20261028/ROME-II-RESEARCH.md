# Rome II / DEI reference research — 28 October 2026

The requested comparison is visual. DEI resource dependencies are useful context for distinct district/building roles, but are not a specification to implement economy, ownership, resources or construction gameplay in this prototype.

## What the sources establish

- [DEI's resource overview](https://divideetimperamod.com/resources/) describes resource-producing regions, regional construction options, resource dependencies and special resource chains. It does **not** establish a one-to-one correspondence between every listed building level and a distinct visible city mesh. That must not be assumed from the building list alone.
- [Creative Assembly's official capital-city presentation](https://wiki.totalwar.com/w/Capital_Cities_(TWR2).html) discusses specially authored **battle maps** for Rome, Athens, Alexandria, Carthage and Babylon. It highlights environmental lighting and detail. Those are not the same scene or asset budget as a campaign settlement. We should learn from their architectural composition while retaining our campaign camera requirements.
- [HD Cities for Rome II, campaign reference](https://www.moddb.com/mods/hd-cities-for-rome-ii/images/with-hd-cities-41) is an identified mod reference, not evidence of unmodified 2013 Rome II. It is useful for the campaign city's dense roof fabric and landmark hierarchy. Its images/assets are not imported into this project.

The references separate the benefits of architectural variety, coherent materials, street frontage, enclosed public space, urban massing, and directional light. Those are art-direction observations, not claims about Creative Assembly's proprietary internal rendering implementation.

## Historical boundary

DEI's list covers buildings outside the 278 BCE showcase date. Its inclusion of a Colosseum or Pantheon does not authorize placing them in this scene. The [King's College London Art of Making monument record](https://aofm-os.kdl.kcl.ac.uk/explore/monuments/113/) dates the Flavian amphitheatre to 70–80 CE. It is excluded.

The [Capitoline Museums' temple account](https://museicapitolini.org/en/sede/campidoglio_antico/tempio_di_giove_capitolino) supports a pre-Republican foundation, dedication in 509 BCE, a deep Tuscan porch, three cellae and terracotta roof decoration. This informs the candidate's architectural language. It does not validate the prototype's illustrative temple placement, exact elevations, domestic details, straight street grid, or full city plan. Those remain interpretive and need historical/art review.

## Acceptance

A passing geometry or integrity check does not mean the result matches Rome II or a 2026 commercial game. Compare the actual matched screenshots, inspect the closer supplementary view and judge architecture, materials, landscape contact and urban composition. Record any remaining grid-like placement, repetition, scale mismatch or unfinished surrounding landscape plainly. Keep the candidate within Roma pending independent review.
